Privacy Policy: 100% Client-Side Guarantee

Zero Server Telemetry • GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliant

Your privacy is not an afterthought—it is the foundational architecture of CALC•. When you use CALC• to plan your mortgage, evaluate debt payoff, track BMI, or calculate ovulation, your personal financial and medical numbers remain strictly in your browser.

1. Zero Personal Data Transmission

Unlike traditional calculation websites, CALC• does not transmit your numerical inputs, salaries, debts, medical statistics, or body measurements to any cloud server or third-party database. All calculation algorithms execute 100% locally inside your client web browser's JavaScript runtime.

2. Local Storage Usage (localStorage)

We use your browser's local storage exclusively for essential user preferences: Day/Night theme mode and temporary calculation history. This data remains on your physical machine and can be cleared at any moment using the "Clear History" button in the history drawer.

3. No Third-Party Trackers or Data Brokers

We do not sell user lead data. When you calculate a $500,000 mortgage or debt consolidation schedule, that information remains strictly on your device and is never bundled as a "lead" to financial sales brokers or lenders.

4. Cryptographic Document Authentication

When a verified PDF calculation report is generated, a unique document reference ID is generated locally using browser cryptographic APIs (crypto.getRandomValues), ensuring authentic client-side verification timestamps without server-side tracking.

5. GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Global Compliance

Because CALC• does not collect, log, track, or sell personally identifiable information (PII), we are inherently compliant with global privacy frameworks including EU GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) - Privacy & Data Security

1. Does CALC• store any personal or financial figures on remote servers?
No. All computational calculations run 100% locally in your web browser. Neither your income, mortgage balance, loan figures, nor health vitals are ever uploaded or saved to remote databases.
2. Does CALC• track user browsing activity or sell data?
No. We maintain an uncompromising Zero Tracking policy. We do not use third-party user fingerprinting, behavioural tracking scripts, or data brokers.
3. How does client-side execution protect my privacy?
By processing formulas directly within the JavaScript engine of your device (client-side sandbox), your sensitive financial or physiological inputs remain strictly within your device's volatile memory.
4. What happens to my data when I export a PDF report?
PDF generation is executed entirely within your browser via HTML5 Canvas and vector generation libraries. No documents are compiled or stored on external cloud infrastructure.
5. Does CALC• comply with GDPR and California CCPA/CPRA?
Yes. Because CALC• does not harvest, store, or process Personally Identifiable Information (PII), we inherently satisfy and exceed the data minimization mandates of GDPR and CCPA.
6. Can network intermediaries or ISPs see my calculation inputs?
No. In addition to high-grade TLS 1.3 encryption across all network transfers, computational values never travel over the HTTP connection because calculations occur locally after page load.
7. What local data is saved in my browser?
Only your personal UI preferences (such as Light/Dark theme selection) and temporary calculation history tape items, stored exclusively in your browser's private localStorage.
8. How can I clear my calculation history and stored preferences?
You can click the "Clear History" button within the History panel at any time, or clear site data in your browser settings to instantly purge all stored records.
